# Как сделать edge-ассистента умнее — отчёт (10 Opus-агентов + синтез)

**Дата:** 2026-06-04 · **Метод:** 10 senior-ML Opus-агентов (по рычагу) + синтез. Стек: on-device Qwen3.5-4B @4-bit, MLX, ru+en, tools+memory.

## Вердикт
> The biggest, cheapest IQ gains are control-flow and grounding over code you already have — promote the intent router to a thinking-depth+escalation controller, add a forced run_python sandbox, grammar-constrain tool calls (llguidance is already installed), and retrieve-not-dump facts — all shippable now; reserve the higher-risk RuadaptQwen3-4B base-swap + DPO agreement-pairs (the only true fix for the morphology slips) for after you build a RuBLiMP eval to measure it, and explicitly skip MLX spec-decode (architecturally blocked) and blanket self-consistency/self-critique (proven net-negative on your 4B).

## Чего НЕ делать (измерено/обосновано)
- Speculative decoding on the MLX path — you already MEASURED it is architecturally blocked on Qwen3.5 (#980, Gated-DeltaNet recurrent state is not trimmable; round-trip identity fails). It is also a pure SPEED lever (lossless, zero IQ gain). Park PLD/spec-decode for the llama.cpp prod path only.
- Blanket self-consistency / best-of-N / multi-agent debate as a default — batch-1 MLX has no parallel-sample speedup, so N is a direct latency+battery multiplier, and a single 4B's samples share the same blind spots. Use vote ONLY on short verifiable outputs (router label, a number), never open-ended replies.

## Полные находки по 10 рычагам

### Russian language quality (SFT / distillation)
**Ключевое:** The "вешаешь / нравится горы" error class is NOT primarily a fine-tuning problem on YOUR side — it is largely a tokenizer + base-model problem that the Russian-LLM community has already solved at exactly your size and family. Vanilla Qwen3.x tokenizes Russian into many sub-word fragments (each Cyrillic word = 2-4 tokens), so the model literally generates morphology fragment-by-fragment with weak agreement signal across the case/gender/number boundary; that is the mechanical root of "нравится горы". RefalMachine's RuadaptQwen3-4B (LEP: Learned Embedding Propagation, arXiv 2412.21140) already swaps in a cl100k+48k-Russian tokenizer, does continued pretraining on a Russian corpus, then propagates embeddings into the instruct model WITHOUT re-doing instruction tuning — ~2x faster Russian generation AND markedly better morphology, Apache-compatible, 4B, MLX-convertible. The single highest-leverage move is to start from RuadaptQwen3-4B as your base (or distill its behavior into your stock Qwen3.5-4B) rather than trying to teach Russian agreement to a fragment-tokenized model with a few thousand LoRA examples. Then a SMALL, targeted SFT/DPO pass (preference pairs of correct-vs-wrong agreement) cleans up the residual slips. Your repo already has the entire MLX LoRA + distillation + fastText-filter pipeline built (lora_qwen4b_polish.yaml, distill_qwen4b.py, quality_filter.py), so the work is data + base-model selection, not infrastructure.

**Топ-пик:** Switch the base to RuadaptQwen3-4B (LEP tokenizer adaptation, arXiv:2412.21140). It attacks the mechanical root cause — Russian fragment-tokenization — that no amount of small-scale LoRA SFT on stock Qwen can fully fix, costs you essentially zero training data, is the same family/size/license, and gives a free 2x ru-decode speedup on-device. If a 4B rev matching your exact Qwen3.5 feature set is unavailable, fall back to text-level distillation from RuadaptQwen3-8B/32B into your stock base, then add the DPO agreement-pairs pass. Build the RuBLiMP/RuCoLA acceptability eval FIRST so you can prove each step moved the agreement number.

**Каверзно:** This lever fixes surface Russian QUALITY — morphology, agreement, register, idiom — and will genuinely remove most 'вешаешь/нравится горы' slips. It will NOT fix the deeper 'feels dumb' problems the project lists as separate weaknesses: multi-turn stateful reasoning, rule/game tracking, and sycophancy are reasoning/control failures, not language failures — a perfectly-conjugated wrong answer still feels dumb. A better Russian model can even make sycophancy WORSE (more fluent caving). Two more honest limits: (1) at 4B and 4-bit, sub-3B-style quantization damage to nuanced ru generation is real — consider 8-bit for the embedding/lm_head layers (Ruadapt's new ru tokens are most sensitive there) even if the rest stays 4-bit. (2) Russian-only SFT/distillation risks catastrophic forgetting of English and of your already-built tool-calling format; always co-train or interleave a slice of your English + tool-call data, and re-run your tool-call and 50-turn dialogue regressions after every ru adapter. Finally, your project scope says ru+en only — none of this addresses Kazakh, which the model-survey memo already flagged as a hole in every base model.

### Reasoning boost at test time (CoT, self-consistency/best-of-N, the enable_thinking dial, self-verify/critique, reasoning distillation) on the on-device Qwen3.5-4B / MLX stack
**Ключевое:** On a batch-1, battery-bound edge assistant the binding constraint is decode latency, not FLOPs — every parallel sample or extra "Wait" multiplies wall-clock and drains battery linearly with no free parallelism (you have no Hexagon-NPU batch trick from arXiv 2509.23324). So the move is NOT "always think more"; it's GATED, ADAPTIVE reasoning: spend a long CoT budget only on the ~10-20% of turns that are genuinely multi-step (math, logic, planning, rule-tracking), and answer the other 80% (companion/fact/command, already classified by your model_router.py) with zero thinking tokens. The single highest-leverage upgrade is wiring your EXISTING enable_thinking dial to the router so effort is chosen per-turn, plus s1-style budget forcing to cap overthinking — which on small models actively degrades accuracy ("Long CoT Degradation", arXiv 2506.07712) and burns battery in reasoning loops.

**Топ-пик:** Router-gated adaptive thinking dial — connect the enable_thinking effort dial you already distilled to the intent labels model_router.py already produces, defaulting to no-think and escalating only on math/multi-step/planning/rule turns, with an s1 hard token cap on the think span. It is low-effort (control-flow over existing code), high-impact (4B small models gain the most from thinking, +15-20% on hard tasks), and costs ZERO extra latency/battery on the 80% of easy turns — the exact opposite of blanket self-consistency.

**Каверзно:** This lever fixes MULTI-STEP and LOGIC reliability (arithmetic, planning, deduction, rule-tracking, tool-arg correctness) — it does little or nothing for three of the four observed 'feels dumb' weaknesses. (1) Russian morphology/agreement is a token-distribution/training problem, not a reasoning-depth problem; more CoT won't fix declension — that needs data/SFT and your deterministic verifier.py, not thinking tokens. (2) Sycophancy is a preference/alignment behavior; CoT can even make it WORSE by letting the model rationalize caving — fix it with anti-sycophancy SFT/DPO, not reasoning. (3) Multi-turn stateful tracking is mostly a context/memory-management problem (what you feed the model across turns) — CoT helps a little but the real fix is your memory layer. Also: parallel TTS (self-consistency/BoN) is fundamentally latency-and-battery-hostile at batch-1 on MLX (no NPU batch parallelism), so it can only be applied rarely and narrowly, never as a default; and on small models long/extended CoT can REDUCE accuracy (Long CoT Degradation) and trigger token-burning reasoning loops, so 'more thinking' must always be capped and gated, never maximized.

### RAG / knowledge grounding
**Ключевое:** The dense-only e5 + exact-cosine DocumentMemory you have is a strong base but is missing the three cheapest, highest-leverage RAG upgrades: (1) a retrieve/no-retrieve GATE so the 4B doesn't pollute factual answers with parametric guesses (and doesn't waste latency retrieving when it shouldn't), (2) HYBRID retrieval + a tiny RERANKER to fix dense-only's blind spot on exact tokens (names, dates, codes, transliterated terms) which is exactly where a small embedder hallucinates the wrong passage, and (3) GROUNDED DECODING with sentence-level citations + an abstain path so "I don't have that in my sources" replaces a confident fabrication. RAG raises *factual* intelligence and slashes hallucination on knowledge the model lacks; it does almost nothing for the reasoning/morphology/sycophancy weaknesses. Critically: with NO general-knowledge KB on-device today, the lever is currently unused for general queries — the single biggest win is shipping a curated offline KB (Wikipedia-RU/EN extract) and wiring the existing e5+DocumentMemory to it.

**Топ-пик:** Retrieval gating + grounded-decoding-with-citations-and-abstain, shipped together over a minimal general-knowledge KB pack. The gate decides when to ground; the KB gives something to ground on (you have none today for general knowledge); the citation+abstain contract (extending your hobby-digest pattern, enforced by verifier.py) converts retrieval into a hallucination/sycophancy reduction. All three are low-effort, reuse e5 + DocumentMemory + verifier.py already built, and need zero new model weights. Add hybrid+RU-lemmatization and the jina reranker as the second wave once the KB exists and bench_retrieval shows the dense-only precision gap.

**Каверзно:** RAG cannot fix the core 'feels dumb' issues — they are not knowledge gaps. Russian morphology/agreement slips are a GENERATION problem (the model still writes the wrong case after reading a correct source); retrieval at best supplies correct surface forms to copy, it doesn't teach declension. Multi-turn stateful reasoning and rule-tracking (games) are WORKING-MEMORY/reasoning failures — RAG feeds context, it doesn't reason over it; a retrieved chess rule won't make the model track board state. Sycophancy is partly mitigated (citations + abstain remove the room to fabricate agreement) but the model can still cave in tone. Generic companion replies are a persona/decoding issue. ### Model selection & scaling
**Ключевое:** For a dense Qwen3.5-4B on-device brain, perceived IQ is gated far more by HOW you train/route the 4B than by swapping it for another base. The single biggest lever is on-policy distillation (student samples its own trajectories, teacher scores them via reverse-KL) from your Qwen3.5-9B hub into the 4B — this is the method Qwen3 itself and Thinking Machines (Oct 2025) used to match RL-level reasoning at a fraction of the cost, and it directly attacks your "feels dumb" failure modes (multi-turn state, rule-tracking) by training the student on the states it actually visits, not teacher-only traces. Second lever is a calibrated escalation router (cascade) to the 9B hub for the ~10-20% genuinely hard queries. Speculative decoding is purely a SPEED lever — it raises tokens/s, never accuracy — but it is what buys you the latency budget to run a "thinking" 4B at a usable speed on a phone. Net: distill for raw IQ, route for the hard tail, spec-decode for speed; do NOT chase a smarter base model — Qwen3-4B-2507/3.5-4B is already the SOTA on-device choice you converged on in project_edge_agent_base_model_choice.md.

**Топ-пик:** On-policy distillation from the Qwen3.5-9B hub into the 4B as a LoRA (reverse-KL / Entropy-Aware OPD), trained on student-sampled rollouts over your real tool-call + multi-turn + ru-morphology task distribution. It is the only technique here that raises raw on-device IQ at zero added inference cost, and it directly targets your three observed weaknesses (multi-turn state, rule-tracking, ru agreement) because the student learns to recover from its own mistaken states rather than imitating clean teacher traces. Your distill_qwen4b.py / distill_high_effort.py already scaffold this — the change is moving from offline teacher-trace SFT to online student-rollout + 9B-scored reverse-KL.

**Каверзно:** This lever cannot make a 4B genuinely match a frontier model — distillation closes the gap on the teacher's distribution but a 4B has a hard capacity ceiling; novel multi-hop reasoning, long-horizon planning, and obscure-knowledge queries will still need the 9B/frontier escalation path, and that path requires network (breaks the offline-first promise for the hard tail). Distillation also cannot inject knowledge the teacher itself lacks: Kazakh is a pretraining-data hole across every candidate base, so no amount of 9B->4B distillation fixes it — that needs continued pretraining or a cloud fallback. Speculative decoding contributes exactly zero to intelligence; it is lossless by construction and only buys latency (do not let it be mistaken for a quality lever). Reverse-KL distillation has a real failure mode — entropy collapse and runaway response length at long context (arXiv 2605.16826, 2603.07079) — so it must be entropy-aware and length-capped or you trade sycophancy for terse, low-diversity, repetitive replies. Finally, a calibrated router only helps if the confidence signal is genuinely calibrated on YOUR workload; an over-eager threshold silently ships easy queries to the cloud (cost + privacy leak), an under-eager one leaves the hard tail feeling dumb — this needs a labeled eval set and periodic recalibration, not a one-time tune.

### Alignment & instruction-following (anti-sycophancy, position-holding, format adherence, calibrated uncertainty)
**Ключевое:** Sycophancy and "caving" are two DIFFERENT failures and need different fixes. (1) Single-turn agree-with-wrong-premise is largely a PROMPT + DECODING problem your stack can fix today with near-zero cost (you already have SYS_BASE doing this weakly). (2) Multi-turn "caves when the user pushes back" is a TRAINED behavioral prior baked into Qwen3.5-4B by its own RLHF/helpfulness tuning — only a lightweight preference-tuning pass (DPO-variant LoRA, hot-swappable like your core/creative adapters) reliably moves it, and the literature shows ~85% sycophancy reduction from as few as ~1k anti-sycophancy preference pairs (IEEE 10825538). The decisive design choice for a 4B is a REFERENCE-FREE objective (ORPO or KTO), because it needs no second resident model — fitting your phone-first, single-resident-base constraint — and KTO needs only binary good/bad labels (no matched pairs), which is far cheaper to author in ru/en than DPO chosen/rejected pairs. Your own measured lesson ("rules beat a model checking itself"; verifier.py) applies directly: do the deterministic/prompt levers first, train only the residual that prompting cannot hold.

**Топ-пик:** Do the serving levers FIRST (technique 1 + 2): harden SYS_BASE into an explicit 3-step epistemic protocol with an adversarial-resistance clause, apply it on ALL analytical paths, and add 2-3 short in-context pushback-recovery exemplars. This is hours of work, fits the phone, and matches your team's proven 'serving-first beats training' discipline. THEN, for the residual multi-turn caving that prompting cannot hold, train one reference-free ORPO (or KTO) anti-sycophancy LoRA via mlx-lm-lora (already venv-compatible), distilling 'chosen=hold-position' answers from your 9B teacher, ~1k ru/en examples, lr 5e-6 with ru replay — hot-swappable exactly like core/creative.

**Каверзно:** This lever changes WHETHER the model holds a correct position and follows format/instructions — it does NOT raise the 4B's underlying competence. It cannot fix: (1) Russian morphology/agreement slips (that's a generation-form problem — your deterministic verifier.py is the right tool, not preference tuning); (2) genuinely-hard reasoning, multi-step math, or rule-tracking for games (the model has to BE right before it can hold a right position — anti-sycophancy on a model that's confidently wrong just makes it stubbornly wrong; that needs tools/escalation, your hub); (3) weak multi-turn stateful reasoning generally (alignment tuning ≠ working-memory capacity). Finally, the dominant cost is authoring high-quality ru/en pushback preference data, not GPU/compute.

### Decoding & structured generation
**Ключевое:** A large fraction of the "feels dumb" failures are not reasoning failures at all — they are FORMAT failures (malformed tool JSON, schema drift, free-text where a slot was expected, runaway repetition, hallucinated tool names). Constrained decoding makes these structurally impossible for ~zero extra compute, because it only masks logits at sampling time. On the MLX stack this is a solved, production-proven path: LM Studio's own MLX engine already ships mlx-lm + Outlines for exactly this. The right framing is: spend the 4B's limited capacity on CONTENT, and let the decoder guarantee FORM. Crucially, constrain only the structural skeleton (keys, enums, types, brackets) and leave free-text fields (the actual "intelligence") unconstrained — over-constraining natural-language fields measurably lowers answer quality.

**Топ-пик:** XGrammar grammar-guided tool-calling + intent routing. It is the highest impact-per-effort move: it eliminates an entire class of hard failures (malformed JSON, hallucinated/unknown tools, missing args, wrong enum labels) deterministically, it is production-proven on the exact MLX stack (LM Studio's MLX engine = mlx-lm + Outlines/structured-gen), it adds negligible per-token cost via cached bitmasks, and it directly hardens the two components already built (tool-calling executor + hybrid intent router). Constrain the skeleton/enums/types only; leave free-text args unconstrained.

**Каверзно:** This lever fixes FORM, not THOUGHT. It cannot make the 4B reason better, plan further, or be less sycophantic in substance — it only guarantees the output is well-formed and within the allowed structure. Specifically it CANNOT fix: (1) Russian morphology/agreement — case and gender concord are multi-token sequence dependencies, not maskable single-token choices; only a finetune/LoRA or grammar-with-morphological-lexicon (impractical at scale) addresses that, logit_bias barely dents it. (2) Multi-turn stateful reasoning — you can externalize state into a forced JSON scratchpad (helps), but the model can still populate it with wrong values; the decoder enforces the slot exists, not that it is correct. (3) Sycophancy / generic replies at the semantic level — sampler diversity and logit_bias only change surface tokens, not the underlying agreeableness in the distribution. (4) Strategy/quality in games — grammars guarantee legal moves, never good ones. Also note over-constraining free-text fields actively LOWERS answer quality (documented in JSONSchemaBench / Tam et al. 2024 'constrained decoding can hurt reasoning'), and constraint masking cannot rescue a base distribution that is wrong — you can force valid JSON full of confidently wrong values. Treat decoding as the cheap reliability floor that lets the genuinely intelligence-raising levers (finetune, RAG quality, escalation) shine, not as an intelligence source itself.

### Agentic orchestration (ReAct, plan-then-execute, self-reflection/critic, verifier passes, decomposition, test-time ensemble, hybrid router as controller)
**Ключевое:** Orchestration's real win on a 4B is GROUNDED LOOPS, not the model talking to itself. Every extra pass on a 4B costs ~0.6-1.5s of decode latency on M3 Pro (and battery on a phone), so the only passes worth paying for are ones whose verdict comes from an external oracle the model itself lacks — a tool result, a parser, code execution, a deterministic checker, or the optional 9B hub. The stack already proved this the hard way: model_verify (4B grading its own grammar) scored 6/8 @ 832ms while the deterministic lexical_fix scored 8/8 @ 0.18ms. Same law applies to self-reflection, debate, and self-consistency: a 4B is a weak verifier of its own reasoning, so loops that close on the model's own judgment add latency and confidence without adding correctness. Reframe the existing hybrid router from an intent classifier into a full CONTROLLER that decides depth, tool, retry, and escalation — that is where this lever pays.

**Топ-пик:** Generalize the single-shot tool call into a bounded (cap=3) tool-grounded ReAct loop, driven by the existing router promoted to a controller. This is the one change that makes the 4B punch above its weight on COMPLEX (compositional, multi-step) requests, because each extra pass closes on an external oracle (tool/observation) rather than the model's own judgment — the only kind of loop that reliably adds correctness instead of just latency on a small model.

**Каверзно:** Orchestration cannot raise the 4B's raw competence ceiling — it can only spend the competence it has more carefully. It will NOT fix Russian morphology/agreement (that's a generation-form issue the deterministic lexical_fix and verbatim-fact recall already handle better than any loop), will NOT make hard reasoning/math/world-knowledge that the 4B simply doesn't know come out right (self-reflection on a wrong-but-confident 4B answer usually keeps the wrong answer — the proven model_verify result generalizes), and will NOT add knowledge — only escalation to the 9B hub or RAG does that. Every loop, vote, plan, and critic pass is paid for in decode latency (~0.6-1.5s/pass on M3 Pro, more on a phone) and battery, so the discipline is to add a pass ONLY when an external oracle (tool, parser, schema, code, hub) supplies the verdict, and to GATE everything behind the controller so the common single-intent turn stays one pass. Sycophancy and generic-companion replies are prompt/SFT/anti-sycophancy problems, not orchestration ones — a critic loop that asks the same sycophantic model 'are you sure?' tends to make it cave further, not less. Self-consistency and multi-agent debate are near-useless here for open-ended answers because a single 4B's samples share the same blind spots; they only help on short verifiable outputs.

### Quantization vs quality + efficiency (Qwen3.5-4B dense hybrid Gated-DeltaNet, MLX on Mac / llama.cpp on iPhone)
**Ключевое:** 4B is in the quantization sensitivity zone where 4-bit DOES cost real reasoning: the empirical Qwen3-quant study (arXiv 2505.02214) shows ~lossless at 8-bit but a consistent 4-bit cliff that grows as models shrink (Qwen3-0.6B loses ~10% MMLU at 4-bit GPTQ vs ~1% for 14B), and Qwen3 is MORE quant-sensitive than Llama3 (C4 PPL 10.4->23.8 for AWQ w3, vs Llama3 9.2->11.6). The "feels dumb" symptoms map onto known PTQ failure modes: math/method/execution errors and early-step CoT flips dominate the damage ("Quantization Meets Reasoning", 2501.03035 / 2505.11574), and Russian morphology/agreement is exactly the low-frequency, outlier-channel behaviour that naive RTN 4-bit smears. The biggest, cheapest win is NOT going to fewer bits but spending bits SMARTER: keep the ~1.8GB text path at a genuinely good 4-bit recipe (calibrated/learned, attention+output+embeddings upcast) instead of a default min-max 4-bit dump, and recover the rest with a tiny 4-bit-aware adapter. Crucial architecture fact: Qwen3.5 is a 3:1 hybrid (only 10/40 layers carry a real GQA KV cache, 2 KV heads; the other 30 are Gated-DeltaNet with a fixed O(1) recurrent state), so KV-cache quantization buys far less memory here than on a plain transformer (user measured 4k->64k ctx = +800MB) AND the DeltaNet state isn't trimmable (mlx-lm #980) -- so spend your effort on WEIGHT-quant quality, not KV bits.

**Топ-пик:** Run mlx_lm.dwq on the 4-bit text path with a custom RU+EN+tool-call calibration set distilled from an 8-bit teacher (--group-size 32, --max-seq-length 512). It is low-effort, offline, drop-in to the existing 2.2GB MLX path, worth ~+0.6 effective bpw of quality, and it directly attacks the Russian-morphology and tool-format degradation because the calibration data finally matches the deployment language/format -- something default English-calibrated quants never fix. Do this before any new training run, then layer the mixed-precision attn/embed upcast and the 4-bit-aware recovery adapter on top.

**Каверзно:** Quantization recipe is a CEILING-PRESERVER, not a ceiling-RAISER. The hard ceiling is 4B parameters: the best possible 4-bit recipe only claws back the gap between a sloppy quant and the bf16 4B (empirically a few MMLU points and the early-CoT-step error spikes) -- it cannot make the model reason like a 9B. So the genuinely weak items in your 'feels dumb' list are NOT primarily quant artifacts and will largely survive a perfect 4-bit recipe: (1) sycophancy/caving is alignment/RLHF behaviour, fix with your anti-sycophancy + DPO, not bits; (2) weak multi-turn stateful reasoning and rule/game tracking is partly the 4B's working-memory limit and partly the mlx-lm #980 multiturn-cache bug -- fix with prompt/scaffolding, llama.cpp serving, and the cloud-9B escalation, not quant; (3) generic-companion replies are a data/persona problem. Quant only meaningfully owns the Russian-morphology slips (outlier-channel smearing -> DWQ + RU calibration + attn upcast genuinely help) and the marginal tool-format/JSON reliability. Also: every quality knob here is offline dev-machine work (DWQ/imatrix/SpinQuant all need a teacher + calibration pass + sometimes a GPU); none runs on the phone, and the phone only ever sees the finished artifact. And note KV quant -- the part of this lever you'd instinctively reach for -- is the LEAST valuable on Qwen3.5 specifically, because the 3:1 hybrid already shrank the KV cache for free.
